Swap vague claims for evidence

'Hardworking and organized' tells little. Explain a real task you completed and how you approached it.

Include transferable skills

School projects, volunteering and family-business responsibilities can show communication, coordination or accuracy. Describe them honestly, without pretending they were paid employment.

Make it relevant

If you want admin work, highlight scheduling and records. If you want customer support, show how you've communicated or solved problems.

REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE

BEFORE: 'Good at organizing.' AFTER: 'Maintained a shared task list for a volunteer event and followed up with team members on deadlines.' Only use examples that are true for you.
