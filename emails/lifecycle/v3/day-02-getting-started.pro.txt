Hi VeeAy! Today we're turning one big job-search challenge into practical steps you can actually use. Keep this guide handy and try the small activity at the end.

Filter 1: Can I apply?

Check country eligibility and schedule requirements. Don't spend an hour tailoring an application for a role that excludes your location.

Filter 2: Can I do the essentials?

Separate 'required' from 'preferred.' If you can demonstrate the essential skills, an unfamiliar preferred tool isn't always a reason to give up.

Filter 3: Is it credible?

Look for a real employer, clear duties and reasonable hiring instructions. Research independently before sharing information.

REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE

Compare three jobs: one strong fit, one stretch opportunity and one clear mismatch. Notice why each belongs in its category.

YOUR TINY WIN

Build a shortlist of three roles and write one reason for each choice.
