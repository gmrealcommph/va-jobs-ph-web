Watch for pressure and payment requests

Upfront fees, 'pay to reserve your slot' demands and rushed decisions deserve serious caution.

Verify the employer

Search independently for the company, compare email domains with official contacts and review whether the role appears on credible company channels.

Protect sensitive information

Don't provide passwords, banking credentials or identity documents without a legitimate, verified reason. Stop if the process feels suspicious.

REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE

A message says you must pay ₱1,500 today for mandatory training before an interview. Pause. Verify through an independent official channel; don't pay simply because the recruiter sounds friendly.
