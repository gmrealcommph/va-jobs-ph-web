Hi VeeAy! Today we're turning one big job-search challenge into practical steps you can actually use. Keep this guide handy and try the small activity at the end.

Choose a direction

Pick two types of roles that suit your current skills and schedule. A job title is only a clue; read the duties and requirements before deciding.

Check eligibility first

Look for location restrictions, time zones, equipment needs and essential experience. Remote doesn't automatically mean open to applicants in the Philippines.

Make a simple tracker

Record the role, employer, link, date and next step. This helps you stay focused and avoid accidentally applying twice.

REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE

Instead of applying to 20 random jobs, shortlist three suitable ones and prepare one thoughtful application.

YOUR TINY WIN

Find one listing that matches your actual experience.
