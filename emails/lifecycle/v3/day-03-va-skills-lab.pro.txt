Understand the role

A virtual assistant may manage email, schedules, documents, research or customer communication. You don't need to learn every task; start with foundational habits.

Practise communication

Write a short email confirming a meeting. Include the date, time zone, attendees and next action. Clear messages are a real workplace skill.

Build a useful system

Create a task tracker with Task, Owner, Deadline and Status. Keep it simple enough that someone else could understand it.

REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE

Practice message: 'Hi Alex, confirming our call on Thursday at 10 AM Manila time. I'll send the meeting link once everyone confirms. Thank you!'
