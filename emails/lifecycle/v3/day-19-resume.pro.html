Clarity beats decoration.

Hi VeeAy! Today we're turning one big job-search challenge into practical steps you can actually use. Keep this guide handy and try the small activity at the end.

YOUR PRACTICAL GUIDE ↓

STEP 01

Lead with relevant facts

Use clear headings, accurate dates and a concise summary. Put the experience most relevant to the role where it's easy to find.

STEP 02

Rewrite weak bullet points

Start with an action. Add context and, if you know it, an outcome. Don't make up achievements or percentages.

STEP 03

Tailor to the role

Read the listing and bring forward the skills you've actually used. Keep a copy of each version you submit.

✍️ REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE

BEFORE: 'Responsible for emails.' AFTER: 'Drafted weekly email updates and organized shared documents for a volunteer project.' Use this only if it reflects your real work.

🌱 YOUR TINY WIN

Improve two résumé bullets using truthful examples.
