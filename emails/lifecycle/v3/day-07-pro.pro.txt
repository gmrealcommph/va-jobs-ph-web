Your job search deserves a system.

Hi VeeAy! Today we're turning one big job-search challenge into practical steps you can actually use. Keep this guide handy and try the small activity at the end.

Five minutes: Find

Scan roles that match your skills, location and preferred hours. Avoid scrolling endlessly.

Ten minutes: Prepare

Choose one strong fit. Tailor a relevant résumé bullet and double-check the employer's instructions.

Five minutes: Track

Record what you applied for, when, and your next step. This simple habit prevents missed follow-ups.

REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE

MONDAY: shortlist two roles. TUESDAY: tailor one application. WEDNESDAY: practise one VA skill. THURSDAY: review your tracker. FRIDAY: evaluate what's working.

YOUR TINY WIN

Try this routine once and adapt it to your schedule.
