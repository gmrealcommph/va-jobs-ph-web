You've learned. Now build momentum.

Hi VeeAy! Today we're turning one big job-search challenge into practical steps you can actually use. Keep this guide handy and try the small activity at the end.

Measure progress beyond offers

Maybe you improved your résumé, learned a new skill or got better at spotting unsuitable jobs. Those changes matter, even if you're still waiting for responses.

Find your biggest obstacle

Not seeing suitable roles? Revisit eligibility filters. Applying without replies? Review fit and application quality. Nervous about interviews? Practise concise examples.

Choose a realistic next goal

Pick one skill to practise, a manageable number of quality applications and a weekly review day. A sustainable plan is better than an impossible one.

REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE

NEXT MONTH: practise spreadsheets twice weekly, shortlist five eligible roles each week, submit three tailored applications and review progress every Sunday. Adjust this to your circumstances.

YOUR TINY WIN

Write down one goal and one action you can take this week.
