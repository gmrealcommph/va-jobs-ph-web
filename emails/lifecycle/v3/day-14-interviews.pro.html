Hi VeeAy! Today we're turning one big job-search challenge into practical steps you can actually use. Keep this guide handy and try the small activity at the end.

YOUR PRACTICAL GUIDE ↓

STEP 01

Start with the situation

Describe a real challenge in one or two sentences. It can come from school, work, volunteering or a personal project.

STEP 02

Explain your action

Say what YOU did, not just what the group did. Focus on the decisions and skills you can honestly discuss.

STEP 03

End with the result

Share what improved or what you learned. You don't need to invent impressive numbers.

✍️ REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE

SITUATION: Project files were scattered. ACTION: I created a shared folder and checklist. RESULT: The team found documents faster and submitted the project on time.

🌱 YOUR TINY WIN

Record one 60-second answer using Situation → Action → Result.
