Hi VeeAy! Today we're turning one big job-search challenge into practical steps you can actually use. Keep this guide handy and try the small activity at the end.

Know when to follow up

Check the employer's instructions first. If there's no stated timeline, a single polite message after roughly one or two weeks may be reasonable.

Keep it brief

Mention the role, your application date and continued interest. Ask for an update without pressure.

Move forward either way

Track your follow-up and keep exploring other roles. Repeated messages rarely improve an application.
