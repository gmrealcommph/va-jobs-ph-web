BRING STORIES TO LIFE THROUGH DESIGN, VIDEO, AND CREATIVITY! Create compelling visual content that brings campaigns to life and connects future students with our brand. Guide, Uplift, and Soar in a #GreatPlaceToWork: Health & Security: HMO (up to 2 dependents) + Life Insurance Growth & Development: Training Programs & Learning Opportunities Work-Life Balance: 30 PTOs and Leave Conversion Family Support: Parental & Maternity Benefits Additional Perks: Transportation, Internet, Clothing, Laundry & Rice Allowances 100% Work From Home: Work remotely from anywhere in the Philippines Work Schedule: Monday to Friday | 8:00 PM to 5:00 AM (PH Time) HAVE WHAT IT TAKES TO BE OUR GRAPHIC DESIGNER / VIDEO EDITOR? 2-3 years of experience in graphic design and video editing , preferably in a marketing, education, or student recruitment environment. Strong portfolio showcasing digital design, social media content, video editing, campaign assets, and print materials . Proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite , particularly Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, Premiere Pro, and After Effects. Strong understanding of Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and other digital content platforms , including formats and creative approaches that drive engagement. Experience creating both short-form and long-form video content for promotional, informational, and campaign purposes. Ability to manage multiple creative projects, prioritize tasks, and meet tight deadlines in a fast-paced environment. Strong attention to detail and commitment to maintaining brand consistency across digital and print materials. Basic knowledge of print production and file preparation for professional printing. Good communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work effectively with marketing and internal teams. Ability to take creative direction while also contributing fresh ideas and proactive creative solutions . Willingness to stay updated on social media trends, design styles, content formats, and video editing techniques . DAY IN THE LIFE OF A GRAPHIC DESIGNER / VIDEO EDITOR Creating Video Content for Digital Campaigns Create and edit engaging short-form videos , including Reels, TikToks, and YouTube Shorts, to support marketing campaigns and increase audience engagement. Edit longer-form videos for promotional, informational, campus, and student-life content. Develop eye-catching video thumbnails, covers, and supporting visual assets optimized for social media platforms. Optimize video formats, dimensions, and outputs for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, websites, and other digital channels. Designing Social Media & Digital Assets Develop creative assets for social media, including static posts, carousels, stories, and campaign visuals . Translate campaign objectives and concepts into visually compelling content that attracts and informs prospective students. Ensure all digital content follows brand guidelines and maintains a consistent visual identity. Collaborate with the marketing team to align creative output with campaign goals, messaging, and brand voice. Producing Print & Marketing Materials Create print-ready materials such as brochures, flyers, posters, event signage, and other promotional collateral . Prepare artwork and files according to professional printing requirements. Maintain consistency across digital and physical marketing materials. Supporting Campaigns & Student Recruitment Contribute creative concepts for marketing campaigns, events, and student recruitment initiatives . Work closely with the marketing team to brainstorm and execute ideas from concept through final production. Create visual content that supports the student journey and strengthens engagement with prospective students. Managing Creative Projects & Deadlines Manage multiple design and video projects simultaneously while meeting established timelines. Prioritize tasks based on campaign requirements and business needs. Incorporate feedback and creative direction while proactively identifying opportunities to improve content. Maintain organized working files and ensure final assets are properly prepared and delivered. Staying Current & Creating On-Site Content Stay informed about emerging social media trends, design styles, editing techniques, and content formats . Identify creative opportunities to keep content fresh, relevant, and competitive. Support on-site content creation when needed, including filming campus activities, events, and student life .
