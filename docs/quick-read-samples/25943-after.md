### About the role

This Senior Executive Assistant role supports the Head of Brand & Customer as a business partner. The aim is to free up the executive’s time by taking ownership of priorities, spotting problems early and keeping work moving without constant direction.

### What you'll actually do

You’ll manage the inbox, plan the calendar 2–4+ weeks ahead, arrange travel and handle personal administration. You’ll also prepare meeting briefings, chase project actions, coordinate marketing suppliers and productions, and improve recurring processes with AI tools and Notion. Clear follow-through matters as much as organising the work.

### Who they're looking for

They want 8+ years of broad business experience, independent judgement, excellent written English and discretion. Practical AI skills and Notion or a similar workflow platform are important, alongside a bachelor’s degree or equivalent professional track record. Senior executive support, marketing coordination and experience in a fast-growing business are desirable.

### Pay & working arrangements

The listed compensation is USD 1,500–3,000 per month. This is a full-time independent contractor engagement requiring strong daily overlap with New Zealand business hours. Exact start and finish times and hiring-country eligibility are not specified in the description.
